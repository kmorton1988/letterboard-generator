Letterboard Letter Generator

text / emoji / images → pegged letters as STL or 3MF
Phrase
One tile per character (spaces & new lines are only used for the board preview). Emoji work. {1}, {2}… insert uploaded images.
Chunky fonts work best – strokes must be wider than the peg.
Letter
Pegs & board
Every letter gets its pegs on the same 8 mm grid measured from the text baseline, so all letters line up on the board.
Images / icons
Output
Letters are laid out face-down (front on the bed, pegs pointing up) so they print without supports and appear mirrored in the 3D view.
